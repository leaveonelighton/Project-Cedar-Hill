Peg & Jerry Gorman

A LEGACY OF PRACTICAL COMPASSION

The Gormans

Peg and Jerry Gorman opened their home to children who needed safety, stability, and hope. Their example reminds us that changing the direction of a life can begin with an ordinary decision to welcome someone in.

Leave One Light On exists because their example did not end at the front door of 5 Cedar Hill Road. It lives on in what we choose to do for one another now.
