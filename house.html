WHERE IT BEGAN

5 Cedar Hill Road

An ordinary house became an extraordinary place of belonging.

The power of the house was never its size or its address. It was the people inside it and the decision to keep the door open.

THE HOUSE

More than an address

5 Cedar Hill Road

For children who needed somewhere safe to land, the Gorman home became a place of stability, welcome, and possibility. It is the place at the center of the story behind Leave One Light On.

The lesson we carry forward is not that everyone must open a foster home. It is that each of us can ask what kind of light we are able to leave on for someone else.
