Find Help

A starting point for practical support.

If you are carrying grief, housing or food insecurity, employment difficulties, recovery needs, family struggles, or another hard season, this page is meant to help you identify the kind of support to look for.

IF YOU NEED HELP RIGHT NOW

Use an established crisis or emergency service.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 in the United States. For mental-health or suicide crisis support, call or text 988 or visit 988lifeline.org. The 988 Lifeline is available 24/7 and provides free, confidential support.

START WHERE YOU ARE

One useful connection can change the next hour, day, or week.

Look for established local organizations and qualified professionals that match your situation. Useful categories can include housing and shelter, food assistance, employment, recovery support, veterans services, family services, education, faith communities, and other community resources.

Leave One Light On is a movement of hope and practical compassion. It is not an emergency-response service and does not provide medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or other professional treatment or advice.

The goal is connection—not pretending one website can solve everything.

LOCAL AND PRACTICAL SUPPORT

Start with 211.

Call 211 or visit 211.org to find local help with housing, food, utility expenses, health care, substance-use support, disaster recovery, and other community needs. Availability varies by location.

Leave One Light On is continuing to review additional resources. Inclusion here does not make Leave One Light On the service provider.

KEEP THE LIGHT ON

Sometimes helping begins by staying present.

If you came here for someone else, listen first. Help them identify what they need, then help them connect with an appropriate local or professional resource.
