# Leave One Light On — Website V3 Review Notes

Status: **Production review complete — August 13, 2026**

## Purpose

Move LeaveOneLightOn.org from book-first presentation to movement-first institutional identity while preserving the origin story and keeping The Light in the Window as the book/story property.

## Locked identity

- Movement: **Leave One Light On**
- Action/support language: **Keep the Light On**
- Book: **The Light in the Window**
- Tagline: **A Movement of Hope and Practical Compassion**

## V3 delivered

- Reworked homepage around Leave One Light On as the primary identity.
- Added restrained metallic-gold treatment and reduced-motion support.
- Repositioned The Light in the Window as the story behind the movement and linked to the independent book site.
- Rebuilt Keep the Light On as an action invitation rather than a competing movement name.
- Replaced under-construction Story, House, and Gormans pages with movement-aligned content.
- Standardized navigation and footer identity across upgraded pages.
- Added a functional Contact route and established-resource guidance through Find Help.
- Added truthful pre-formation organizational-status language without claiming current 501(c)(3) recognition or tax deductibility.
- Added canonical metadata, `robots.txt`, and a nine-page `sitemap.xml`.
- Preserved the legacy `book.html` and `resources.html` URLs with exact-path permanent redirects.

## Production closeout

Desktop/responsive presentation, internal links, external book/help destinations, contact information, images, canonical routes, redirect behavior, robots, and sitemap were verified after deployment. Google Search Console successfully processed the current sitemap and reported nine discovered pages.

The original pre-merge hold is closed. Future website work should use a new scoped branch and receive owner approval before merge when it can trigger Hostinger auto-deployment.
