THE ACTION BEHIND THE MOVEMENT

Keep the Light On

You do not have to solve everything to make a difference.

Sometimes it begins by noticing one person, opening one door, making one call, sharing one trustworthy resource, or simply staying present.

WHAT IT MEANS

Leave One Light On is the movement.

Keep the Light On is the invitation.

I was once the person searching for the light. Peg and Jerry Gorman left theirs on for me.

Keeping the light on means carrying that kind of practical compassion forward in whatever responsible way is yours to give.

Welcome before judgment. Hope before outrage.
