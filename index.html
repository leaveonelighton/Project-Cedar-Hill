WHY THIS STORY MATTERS

The story began with a house.

The movement begins with what we do next.

Some people change the world without ever becoming famous.

Jeremy "Jerry" and Margaret "Peg" Gorman were two of those people.

At thirteen years old, I arrived at 5 Cedar Hill Road searching for a place to belong.

I found something far greater.

I found a family.

Leave One Light On honors what Peg and Jerry gave so freely and asks a larger question: who can each of us notice, welcome, encourage, mentor, or stand beside today?

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE LIGHT

The Gormans

Ordinary people. Extraordinary compassion.

Peg and Jerry Gorman quietly opened their home to children who needed safety, stability, and hope. Their lives remind us that changing the world often begins by opening a single door.

LEAVE ONE LIGHT ON

Someone left a light on for me.

Now it's my turn to leave one on.
